🐟 Fishman v16 · 1–4 Player Co-op

Team up for all 10 chapters and the nine-boss arena! P1 hosts; up to three friends join on their own devices with the same room code. Each hero has independent movement, aim, weapons, health, lives and skills.

Create a room or enter a friend's code. No account or download needed.

Waiting for teammates…

Play with 1–4 people, each on a different device. Anyone can advance the group by pressing B at a completed exit gate. Everyone shares goals and rewards. Revive nearby allies by pressing B. Boss health scales with the team. Uses WebRTC via the public PeerJS signalling server; room codes are invitations, not passwords, and certain networks may block connections. The room ends if P1 leaves.
